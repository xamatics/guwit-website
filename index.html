Why language

Most software has never heard your language.

Gungbe is spoken every day across Badagry and into the Republic of Benin. Nigerian Pidgin is spoken by tens of millions. Neither is well served by the tools everyone else builds on — there is little training data, few dictionaries in digital form, and almost no software that treats them as first-class.

We work on that gap directly: teaching apps, recorded audio, glossaries and translation for organisations that need to reach people in the language they actually think in.

Our language services

  • Translation — documents, health and public information, product copy and app strings, into and out of Gungbe, Nigerian Pidgin, Yoruba and English.
  • Audio and transcription — native-speaker recordings and written transcripts, useful for teaching material and for language datasets.
  • Localisation — adapting an interface so it reads naturally, rather than translating word by word into something nobody says.
  • Teaching material — structured lessons and vocabulary, of the kind that sits behind our own Learn Gungbe app.
