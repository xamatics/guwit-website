Services

What you can hire us to do.

Four core services. Each one is quoted as a fixed scope with a fixed price before any work begins.

Service 01

Apps & software development

Android applications and the server systems behind them, designed for mid-range phones, small storage and expensive data.

AndroidPlay StoreAPIsDashboards

What this includes

  • Android applications, published to Google Play under your account or ours.
  • Offline-first design, so the app keeps working when the network does not.
  • Backend systems, databases and admin dashboards.
  • Integration with payment, messaging and mapping services.
  • Store listing, screenshots, policy pages and the review process.
  • Updates and maintenance after launch.
Service 02

Websites, design & hosting

Sites that load quickly on a phone, read well to a screen reader, and can be found on Google. Designed and built from scratch, not assembled from a template.

DesignBuildHostingSEO

What this includes

  • Business websites, landing pages and product sites.
  • Visual identity work: logo, colour, type and brand guidelines.
  • Domain registration, DNS setup, hosting and SSL.
  • Search engine fundamentals: structured data, sitemaps, metadata.
  • Accessibility to WCAG standards, tested with a keyboard and a screen reader.
  • Ongoing hosting, backups and content updates.
Service 03

WhatsApp & conversational systems

Most Nigerian customers will not install an app to place one order. They will send a WhatsApp message. We build complete business systems that run inside that conversation.

WhatsApp platformAutomationPaymentsLogistics

What this includes

  • Guided ordering flows with menus, options and a running cart.
  • Automatic pricing, order numbers and payment confirmation.
  • Supplier or vendor notifications, with one-tap confirmation.
  • Dispatch and assignment logic, including routing by zone.
  • Location sharing, delivery updates and post-delivery ratings.
  • An operator view so your staff can step into any conversation.
Service 04

Translation & language services

Translation and localisation for low-resource West African languages — Gungbe and Nigerian Pidgin in particular — done by native speakers who also understand the technical side.

GungbeNigerian PidginYorubaEnglish

What this includes

  • Document translation: health, public information, legal, educational and commercial.
  • Software and app localisation, including interface strings and store listings.
  • Native-speaker audio recording, with written transcripts.
  • Glossaries and terminology lists for organisations working in these languages.
  • Language data collection and preparation for research and machine learning use.
  • Subtitling and voice-over for video and radio material.
