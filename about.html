About us

A software company that took the language problem seriously.

Guwit Technology Solutions Ltd is a private company registered in Nigeria, working from Badagry, Lagos State.

Guwit builds software for Nigerian businesses and organisations: Android applications, websites, and automated systems that run on WhatsApp. Alongside that, we do something most development companies do not — we work on languages that technology has largely ignored.

Where we work from

Badagry sits at the western edge of Lagos State, on the border with the Republic of Benin. It is a town with its own languages, its own trade, and businesses that are underserved by software written for Lagos Island or for somewhere else entirely. Building here means designing for real conditions: mid-range Android phones, expensive data, patchy power, customers who will not install an app for a single transaction.

Those constraints have made us better engineers. A system that works well in Badagry works anywhere.

What we believe

  • Build for the phone people actually have. Not the newest device on the newest network.
  • Meet people where they already are. A WhatsApp conversation will beat a beautiful app that nobody downloads.
  • A language with few speakers online still deserves software. Low-resource does not mean low-value.
  • Say what something costs, before building it. Fixed scope, fixed price, written down.

Language work

Gungbe — the language of the Ogu people of Badagry and southern Benin — has almost no presence in digital tools. There are few dictionaries in machine-readable form, little recorded audio available for learning, and no mainstream software that supports it. The same is true, at a different scale, of Nigerian Pidgin: enormously spoken, barely supported.

We publish Learn Gungbe on Google Play, and we take on translation, transcription and localisation work in these languages for clients who need to communicate with people properly rather than approximately.
